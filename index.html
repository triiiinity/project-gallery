about

project gallery is an online gallery that was established in october 2020.

pg hosts curated exhibitions organized by Trinity Lester. pg is based in new york city but functions primarily online, with select infrequent in-person exhibitions in brooklyn, ny.

project gallery is going through website updates. we are in the process of moving our older exhibitions into an archive on the new site. please be patient, and check that your old links still work if they are on your cv or elsewhere.
